Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Budget Buddy ("the app", "we", "our") is a personal finance tracking tool. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and what rights you have over it. It's written in plain language rather than legal boilerplate, because that's more useful to actually read.

Before publishing: replace the contact details in the "Contact Us" section below with your real name/business and an email address you actually check. This is drafted for a single developer running their own app for personal or small-scale use — if the app is ever offered to the public at scale, it's worth having this reviewed by someone who does POPIA/data-protection compliance professionally.

1. What Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (the password itself is never stored or visible to us — it's handled entirely by Supabase, our authentication provider, using industry-standard hashing).
  • Financial data you enter: income, expense, asset and liability entries, including descriptions and amounts; loan details you provide (loan type, interest rate, principal, term, start date); and the names and balances of any bank or savings accounts you add.
  • Usage data: basic technical data such as login timestamps, generated automatically by our hosting providers (Vercel and Supabase) for security and reliability — we don't run any separate analytics or tracking on top of this.

We do not collect your real bank login details, card numbers, or ID numbers, and the app never asks for them — entries are just numbers and descriptions you type in yourself.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's core functionality: storing your entries, calculating balances, and showing them back to you when you log in.
  • To calculate loan figures (monthly payments, remaining balances, effective rates) from the numbers you provide and the current prime interest rate.
  • To authenticate you securely and keep your data separate from any other user's.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell or rent it to third parties, and do not use it to generate personalised financial advice or recommendations — the app performs calculations based on the figures you enter; it does not tell you what to do with your money. See the disclaimer in Section 6.

3. Who Can See Your Data

Your financial entries are private to your account. The app uses row-level security at the database level, meaning the system itself enforces that your data is only ever readable by your own logged-in session — not by other users of the app, and not by us browsing the database directly in the normal course of running it.

Two service providers process data on our behalf, as is normal for any web app:

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles authentication.
  • Vercel — hosts and serves the app itself.

Neither is given access to your data beyond what's needed to host and run the app.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual entries, accounts, or loans at any time from within the app. To delete your account entirely, contact us using the details below and we'll remove your account and all associated data.

5. Your Rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information of yours is held and how it's used (this policy).
  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to any legal requirement to retain it.
  • Object to how your information is processed.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 8.

6. Not Financial Advice

Budget Buddy performs calculations (such as loan payments, interest projections, and balance tracking) based entirely on the numbers you provide. It does not analyse your circumstances or recommend a course of action, and nothing in the app should be treated as financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Always use the actual figures quoted to you by a bank or lender, and consult a qualified professional for advice specific to your situation.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) between your device and our servers. Access to your data requires your account password, and the database enforces per-user access at the row level as described in Section 3. No system is perfectly secure, but no unnecessary data is collected or stored beyond what the app needs to function.
